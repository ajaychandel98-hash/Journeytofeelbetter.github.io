A little place of light — train and Panch Kedar edition

Upload index.html and the complete media folder to GitHub Pages, keeping all paths. Media files are JavaScript packets containing the MP4 bytes; they play through browser Blob URLs without byte-range streaming.
Seventeen videos are included. The previous horizontal Shimla clip is removed. New clips cover all five Panch Kedar temples, the Kalka–Shimla railway and a portrait Shimla evening.
Videos attempt audible autoplay after navigation; browsers may require muted fallback. Holding Shiva’s hand starts Namami Shamishan through YouTube. Temple videos stay muted while the mantra plays; their sound button switches to original clip audio.
