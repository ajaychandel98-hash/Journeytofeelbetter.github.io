A little place of light

To share the website, keep index.html and the entire media folder together.
The three Shiva videos are bundled at media/shiva/shiva-1.mp4 through shiva-3.mp4.
They do not require access to the original phone gallery or a separate storage account.
Upload the contents of this folder to GitHub Pages or another static website host.
For GitHub Pages, index.html should be in the root of your selected publishing folder.
Do not upload just index.html: the media folder is required for the Shiva videos.

Shiva clips autoplay muted where the browser permits. Use Turn on video sound to hear them.
The music player, fonts and other categories' linked photographs require internet access.
